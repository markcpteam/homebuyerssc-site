The risk in a relocation is not selling for slightly less. It is paying for two places at once, for longer than you planned, because a sale you were counting on did not finish.

Nationally, 7% of contracts were terminated in the three months to August 2026 and 14% had delayed settlements, according to the National Association of Realtors. On a normal sale that is an inconvenience. On a relocation it is two mortgages, or a mortgage and a lease, for months you did not budget.

Homebuyers SC sets the closing date to suit the seller, up to twelve months out, across Charleston, Berkeley, and Dorchester counties.

## What does a relocation actually cost if the sale slips?

Add your own numbers, because the total surprises people.

| Cost while both places are yours | Notes |
|---|---|
| Mortgage here | Continues until closing |
| Rent or mortgage there | Starts on your report date, not on your sale date |
| Property taxes and insurance here | Continue, and a vacant house may be repriced or non-renewed |
| Utilities here | You cannot switch them off. A Lowcountry house needs air conditioning running |
| Maintenance and yard here | Now at a distance, usually paid for |
| Travel back | For showings, repairs, closing, or all three |
| Time | Managing a sale remotely, while starting a new job |

The last two rows are the ones people forget. Managing a listing from another state, during the first months of a new job, is the part that wears people down more than the money.

## Should I list it or sell it outright?

The honest answer depends on your timeline and the condition of the house.

| | List it | Sell to a cash buyer |
|---|---|---|
| Likely gross price | Higher | Lower |
| Certainty of closing | 7% of contracts terminate nationally | A set date, with the closing attorney confirming title |
| Time to funds | Days on market, plus 30 to 45 days for a lender | Usually 1 to 3 weeks, or a date you pick |
| Cost before you are paid | Repairs, staging, carrying both places | None |
| Managing it from out of state | Showings, repairs, and decisions at a distance | One walkthrough |
| Fits when | Good condition, time before your report date, and you can carry both | A hard date, or a house that needs work |

**List it if** the house is in good condition, a lender would finance it today, and you have enough runway that a failed contract would not be a crisis. The higher price usually survives.

**Sell outright if** your report date is fixed, you cannot comfortably carry both places, or the house needs work you will not be there to manage. The arithmetic is in [cash offer vs listing with an agent in Charleston](/guides/cash-offer-vs-realtor-what-you-net-in-south-carolina/).

## Can I sell from out of state?

Yes, and it is routine. One thing to know so nobody promises you the wrong thing.

South Carolina requires closings to be supervised by a licensed South Carolina attorney, from *State v. Buyers Service Co.*, 292 S.C. 426, 357 S.E.2d 15 (1987). South Carolina does **not** currently permit remote online notarization, so you cannot sign over video. What you do instead is sign in front of a notary physically present with you, wherever you are, and return the documents by overnight courier. Funds are wired.

If you will be a nonresident at the time of sale, expect withholding at closing under SC Code 12-8-580. Furnishing a gain affidavit means it is calculated on the gain rather than the full amount realized, which is usually a large difference in cash at closing.

*Withholding and tax outcomes depend on your own return. Confirm with a CPA, and confirm the closing mechanics with your closing attorney.*

More detail in [selling an inherited house when you live out of state](/guides/selling-an-inherited-property-out-of-state-without-the-hassle/), which covers the same remote-closing mechanics.

## What if my employer is offering relocation assistance?

Read what it actually covers before deciding anything. Some packages cover closing costs, some cover a period of duplicate housing, and some include a guaranteed buyout with conditions attached. A buyout offer is worth comparing against a cash offer and against a listing, on net rather than on headline price.

Ask specifically what happens if the house does not sell by a certain date, because that clause is where the value is.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I time the sale against my start date?**

Backwards from the start date. Decide what date you need to be free of the house, then choose the route that can hit it. A closing date you set is worth more than a higher price you might get.

**Can I close after I have moved?**

Yes. You do not need to be in South Carolina, and you do not need to be in the house.

**What about my belongings?**

On a cash sale you can leave what you do not want. See [selling an inherited house full of belongings](/guides/selling-a-property-after-inheriting-a-messy-estate-how-to-simplify-the-chaos/).

**What if I already listed it and nothing happened?**

That is information about the house rather than about the market. See [selling a house after a failed listing](/guides/selling-a-house-after-a-failed-listing-what-to-do-when-the-market-says-no/).

**What if I lost the job rather than moved for one?**

**How fast can it close?**
