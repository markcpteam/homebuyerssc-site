Homebuyers SC buys houses in James Island as-is for cash, with no repairs, no showings, no commission, and a closing date you choose.

James Island is split between the Town of James Island, the City of Charleston, and unincorporated Charleston County, so two houses on the same road can answer to different permit offices.

## Which county is your James Island property in?

The county decides which offices you deal with, and on some James Island addresses the answer is not obvious.

| What you need | Where it lives |
|---|---|
| Probate, if the owner has died | Charleston County Probate Court |
| Recorded deeds, liens, judgments | Charleston County Register of Deeds |
| Delinquent property taxes | Charleston County delinquent tax office |

**Insurance note.** The South Carolina Wind and Hail Underwriting Association, the state's market of last resort for wind and hail coverage, operates in designated coastal zones of Charleston County. It covers wind and hail only, not fire, liability, or flood, and it carries condition requirements including a sound roof. Confirm eligibility for your address with an insurance agent.

## What comes up most on James Island houses

- **Which jurisdiction you are in** decides who handles permits and code enforcement. Confirm it for your specific address rather than assuming.
- **Flood and marsh proximity.** Properties near the marsh face both flood exposure and the wind and hail question.
- **Older ranch housing** across much of the island, with the systems and roof age that go with it.
- **Crawlspace moisture and settlement** are the routine findings here.

One walkthrough, a written offer with proof of funds usually within 24 to 48 hours, then an attorney-supervised closing on a date you pick, typically one to three weeks once title clears, or up to twelve months out if that suits you better. South Carolina requires a licensed attorney to supervise every closing, under *State v. Buyers Service Co.*, 292 S.C. 426, 357 S.E.2d 15 (1987).

## Should you sell to a cash buyer at all?

Often not. If the house is in good condition, a lender would finance it today, and you have time to wait, listing it will usually net you more. We would rather tell you that than write an offer you should not take. The arithmetic, including the case for listing, is in [cash offer vs listing with an agent in Charleston](/guides/cash-offer-vs-realtor-what-you-net-in-south-carolina/).

A cash sale tends to fit when the house needs work a lender will not accept, you are against a deadline, the property is tenant-occupied or in an estate, or a listing has already failed.

## Questions we get about James Island property

**How do I find out whether I am in the town, the city, or the county?**

Ask the county, or check your tax notice, which shows the taxing jurisdiction. It decides who holds the permit record and who handles code enforcement, and two neighbours can have different answers.

**What if the property backs onto marsh?**

Common here, and it brings both flood and moisture considerations. We buy marsh-front and marsh-adjacent property.

**The house is an older ranch that has never been updated. Will you buy it?**

That is a large share of what we buy. Original systems and an aged roof are pricing factors, not disqualifiers.
