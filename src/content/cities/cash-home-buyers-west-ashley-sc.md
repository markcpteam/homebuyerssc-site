## Which county is your West Ashley property in?

The county decides which offices you deal with, and on some West Ashley addresses the answer is not obvious.

| What you need | Where it lives |
|---|---|
| Probate, if the owner has died | Charleston County Probate Court |
| Recorded deeds, liens, judgments | Charleston County Register of Deeds |
| Delinquent property taxes | Charleston County delinquent tax office |

**Insurance note.** The South Carolina Wind and Hail Underwriting Association, the state's market of last resort for wind and hail coverage, operates in designated coastal zones of Charleston County. It covers wind and hail only, not fire, liability, or flood, and it carries condition requirements including a sound roof. Confirm eligibility for your address with an insurance agent.

## What comes up most on West Ashley houses

- **Mid-century housing stock** in much of the area, which means original wiring, original plumbing, and roofs at or past the end of their lives in houses that have not been updated.
- **Drainage and flooding** are a real issue in specific pockets, and repeated water claims make a house harder to insure.
- **City of Charleston permitting**, not Charleston County, for most of West Ashley. That matters when you are checking whether an addition was permitted.
- **Crawlspace moisture** is common, and it is the finding that most often surprises sellers at inspection.

## Should you sell to a cash buyer at all?

Often not. If the house is in good condition, a lender would finance it today, and you have time to wait, listing it will usually net you more. We would rather tell you that than write an offer you should not take. The arithmetic, including the case for listing, is in [cash offer vs listing with an agent in Charleston](/guides/cash-offer-vs-realtor-what-you-net-in-south-carolina/).

A cash sale tends to fit when the house needs work a lender will not accept, you are against a deadline, the property is tenant-occupied or in an estate, or a listing has already failed.

## Questions we get about West Ashley property

**Is West Ashley handled by the city or the county?**

Most of West Ashley is inside the City of Charleston, so city permitting and city code enforcement apply. That is where the permit record for your address lives.

**The street floods but the house never has. Does that matter?**

To a financed buyer's insurer, the flood zone matters more than your experience of it. Get the zone for the parcel, not the street's reputation.

**What if the roof is original?**

Roof age is the single most common insurance trigger in this market. It does not stop us buying, and it is often what stops a retail sale.
