## What sellers most often mention

These are the themes that come up repeatedly in the reviews below, not a list Homebuyers SC wrote about itself.

- **Communication throughout.** The most common thing sellers mention is being kept informed at every step rather than chasing someone for an update.
- **Straight answers.** Sellers describe the negotiation as direct and honest, including when the answer was not what they hoped.
- **Flexibility on the closing date.** Several sellers changed their closing date, in one case repeatedly, and in another delayed roughly two months so a long-term tenant could find housing.
- **Care in difficult situations.** Sellers handling a parent's house or an estate mention the situation was treated as more than a transaction.
- **Payment arrives when promised.**
